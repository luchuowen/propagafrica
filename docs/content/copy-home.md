## 3. The first four weeks — the full-width figure block

This is the page's largest drawing. Figure to the left, words to the right, sheet footer beneath.

Eyebrow `THE FIRST FOUR WEEKS`

H2 **Grafting joins the top of one plant to the roots of another.**

Body
For about four weeks that join has to be held still, kept damp and kept clean while it knits together and puts out roots. Most of the plants a nursery loses are lost in that window, and the loss is invisible until the count at the end of it.

Second paragraph
Every item we sell does one job in those four weeks. That is why the catalogue is arranged the way the work is, and why the sleeve you order is chosen against a measured stem rather than a product name.

Mono line, `--green`
`SIZE IT AGAINST A MEASURED STEM, NOT AGAINST A NAME`
